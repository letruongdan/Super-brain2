## Finding 1 (P2) — `prepare_task` Degraded Codes for `discover` and `work`

### 1. Invariant
Every degraded-capability, truncation, stale-observation, and unreviewed-state code returned by a partial-domain entrypoint must match the published contract taxonomy and be committed durably alongside the delivery outcome so initial responses, direct reads, and replays agree across state transitions.

### 2. Sibling Sweep
- **`prepare_task(mode="discover")` ([`handoff_service.py::execute_readonly`](../../src/superbrain/application/handoff_service.py#L188-L222)):** Previously emitted `"handoff_discover_truncated"` when `select_discover_items` dropped items under `context_budget_tokens`. Switched to `_CONTINUITY_TRUNCATED_CODE` (`"continuity_truncated"`) per §7.4 and §20 case 58, alongside `_UNAVAILABLE_SECTION_CODES` (`"skills_unavailable"`, `"lessons_unavailable"`, `"context_unavailable"`, `"finishing_gates_unavailable"`). Verified both open-checkout listing (`task_id=None`) and direct `task_id` lookup (sufficient and insufficient budgets) share the exact same `select_discover_items` whole-item truncation path and code.
- **`prepare_task(mode="work")` snapshot & unbuilt section codes ([`handoff_service.py::_prepare_work_task`](../../src/superbrain/application/handoff_service.py#L233-L326)):** Previously omitted `_UNAVAILABLE_SECTION_CODES` whenever returning empty `skills: []`, `lessons: []`, `context: []`, `finishing_gates: []`. Fixed both `source_snapshot_id is not None` (`snapshot_status="available"`) and `source_snapshot_id is None` (`snapshot_status="unavailable"` -> includes `"snapshot_unavailable"`) paths to always emit `_UNAVAILABLE_SECTION_CODES`, preserving `oneOf` snapshot invariants in `schemas/mcp-tools-v2.json` and matching §7.5 / §20 case 43. Note: [`_render_delivery`](../../src/superbrain/application/handoff_service.py#L604-L625) reads committed `rec.degraded_codes` directly without synthesis so fresh and replay return the exact committed list.
- **`open_handoffs_present` and 14-day recency window parity ([`handoffs.py::tx_record_work_delivery`](../../src/superbrain/storage/handoffs.py#L1354-L1480) & [`handoffs.py::has_open_handoffs`](../../src/superbrain/storage/handoffs.py#L315-L328)):**
